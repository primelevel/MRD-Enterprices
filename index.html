Mobile fridge hire

Refrigeration that comes to your event or business.

Cold storage for events and functions

A mobile fridge is a refrigerated trailer that can be brought to a venue, so food and drinks stay cold without relying on the venue's own fridges.

MRD Enterprises hires these out for events and functions. Business enquiries for refrigeration may also be considered.

How to enquire

  • Send us your event date and location.
  • Tell us what you need to keep cold and roughly how much.
  • We reply with availability and pricing. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
